### audio_index.json: not drift, verified by design

The P3 audio item's "289 sermons" is the numbered church-sermon corpus,
the same 289 that make up `assets/sermons/en/` — not the full 429-record
corpus. The 140 `fy-`-prefixed 福音電台 records (including the 15 that
`f25ecd06` calls "audio-only messages") are a different source with no
representation in `audio_index.json` at all, and that is intentional:
`test/sermon_list_audio_signal_test.dart` pins `playable == 289` against
a 429-sermon corpus with the comment "the corpus grew to 414 on
2026-09-06 and the audio index did not; if either moves, read why before
editing this." Ran that test today — still green, still 289/429. The
"15 audio-only messages" in `f25ecd06`'s title describes the *source*
material (福音電台 broadcasts that only ever existed as audio, as
opposed to the church's own recorded/video sermons), not a claim that
those 15 gained an app-playable recording. No action needed; reported
because it looked like a mismatch until checked against the test that
already pins it.
