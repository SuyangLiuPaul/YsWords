## Top finding: one letter figure has drifted

`docs/梁家鏗譯本-請教出版方.md` §四 says **7,788 identical / 86 wording
differences**. A fresh run of `tools/proofread_biblexg.py` today gives
**7,786 identical / 88 wording differences** (comparable count and
punctuation-only count are unchanged: 7,920 and 46).

**Root cause, not a mystery**: commit `33304965` (2026-09-02, "路加福音
23:34a becomes a verse you can select") split 路加福音 23:33 into a
standalone 23:33 and a new 23:34a. Before the split, our 23:33 held the
publisher's full combined text and compared as *identical*. After the
split, our 23:33 is shorter than the publisher's single combined verse,
so it now compares as a *wording difference*. That accounts for one of
the two newly-diverged verses; I did not track down the second by hand,
but `git log` confirms **no commit has touched
`assets/biblexg-v2.json`/`assets/biblexg-v2-tr.json`/`tools/proofread_biblexg.py`
since the letter's number was written** (commit `8575f4b8`, 2026-09-03
22:01, itself *after* the split commit) — so whatever produced 7,788/86
in the letter did not match what the tool produces on that same
commit's tree today. The letter's number is stale relative to its own
cited assets, not something that drifted afterward.

This doesn't change what the letter is asking the publisher (the
question is still "which of these differences is a later revision"),
but the letter should quote 88, not 86, and 7,786, not 7,788, before it
is sent. **Not fixed here** — editing the letter's numbers is a content
decision for whoever sends it, and this task's brief was measure-only.

### H3069/H3068: a one-off reproduction attempt, not a clean match

No committed tool computes this figure — the queue entry (line 2982-2990)
describes a "fresh sweep" done inline, not saved as a script. I wrote a
throwaway (not committed) comparison of `assets/tagged/cuvs-yhwh/*.json`
against `assets/originals/*.json`, keyed on `book, chapter:verse`, and got:

- 281 verses where the corpus's H3069 tag agrees with an H3069 also present in `assets/originals/`
- **56** verses where the corpus tags H3069 and the originals verse contains H3068 but no H3069 at all
- **12** verses where the originals verse (by the same key) contains neither — matches the letter's 12 exactly
- **2** verses (Ezekiel 20:47, 20:49) where the key doesn't exist in `assets/originals/` at all

That's 56 vs. the cited 57, and 2 unresolved keys the letter's count
apparently absorbed somewhere I didn't reproduce. Ezekiel 20:45-49
(English) is 21:1-5 in Hebrew versification — exactly the "Ezekiel
versification offset" the queue entry calls out by name — so my
straight key lookup almost certainly missed a chapter-boundary
re-mapping that the original count handled correctly. **`git log`
confirms neither `assets/tagged/cuvs-yhwh/` nor `assets/originals/` has
changed since the letter was drafted**, so this 1-2 verse gap is a
methodology difference in my reproduction, not corpus drift. I'm not
confident enough in my version to call 69 wrong, and not confident
enough in 69 to call my 68/70 wrong either — flagging as **inconclusive**
rather than asserting either number.

**If this figure needs to survive future re-verification, it's worth a
real script** rather than redoing this ad hoc each time — added to the
queue below.
