All your training, all the physical training that you have done, whether it be in the martial arts or in sports, you feel in your muscle and every fiber the strength. You back down from nothing. That strength — you know you have more than enough to meet the challenge. But more important than that is the spiritual power that every Christian should have. When you are filled with the Spirit, you have a sense of power when you have to do his work. You have also with it the sense of love, because we are not non-Christians anymore. We do not fight in a carnal way. We do not fight with hatred. These are important things.

And so, you feed upon the flesh of Christ until the power of sin is sensibly broken in yourself. And then what do you do? You feed upon his word. Now, the word of God is vast. We have been expounding it into our seventh year. So which part of it? Now here is where it is so important to understand John 14, verse 26: "The Holy Spirit will bring his word to your remembrance." He will bring that word to your remembrance. He will remind you of it. He will apply some part of the Lord's teaching that is suitable to you for that moment, for that day. Let the Holy Spirit bring that word to you and you feed upon it. You see, the beauty of a spiritual life is you are simply open to God, and he, being the living God, brings to your heart and to your mind the passage suitable to you at that time, and you feed upon it. You start meditating upon it. You ask the Lord to teach you what that means as applying to yourself, that particular day, that particular moment.

Now time is easily going. Let's come quickly to the fourth and last point here. We are to be filled with something else. We are to be filled with God's will — with the knowledge of his will. Colossians chapter 1, verse 9. Just look speedily at Colossians chapter 1, verse 9. And this is what we read here: "And so, from the day we heard of it" — that is, we heard of your faith, of your love — "we have not ceased to pray for you, asking" — notice again this has to do with prayer — "asking that you may be filled with the knowledge of his will in all spiritual wisdom and understanding." Filled with what? With the knowledge of his will. Oh, that's wonderful.

It is a sign of a weak and diseased Christianity that forever needs to have a sort of "how-to-do-it-yourself" lesson on discovering the will of God. We are looking for ways: "I don't know the will of God, so how do I find the will of God?" Well, here he is saying that you may be filled with the knowledge of his will. Now, is that your experience? Or are you forever trying to figure out, "Is that God's will, or is it not God's will?" How to live a Christian life like this? You never know whether it's God's will or not God's will. And then you are trying to figure out which way to discover. And some people have even worked out six or seven points on how to discover the will of God. Techniques for discovering the will of God. Well, there isn't much of a technique to begin with. You just get yourself filled, by the grace of God, by his gift to you. Filled with the Spirit of God and you will soon know his will.

But my question is: do you live by his will? Do you even bother to know what his will is? We have a church today in which, when I talk to many Christians, it becomes clear to me that they neither live in the center of God's will, nor do they know what that will is, nor are they very concerned to discover how to live in that will. They only want to know whether it's God's will if they have to make a major decision. And the rest of the time they can do whatever they like. And so when they come to a crisis, they want to say, "What is God's will?" And they don't know how to discover his will. Unless you live constantly in his will, don't bother to find out what his will is in a crisis, because the likelihood is, he won't even tell you. If you most of the time don't live in the center of his will, how are you going to live in the center of his will when a crisis comes? Because by that time you are so far off from whatever his normal will for you might be, that how is that particular crisis going to fit into the course of events as God plans it for your life?

But he who daily communes with the Lord, who daily feeds upon the spiritual goodness of the Lord, will be filled with his will. Now in the Bible, the amazing thing is that even God's will — this fourth thing — is compared to food. Where do we find this? Take for example John chapter 4, verse 32. Let me read to you John 4:32 and 34. John 4:32, the Lord says this, when he said to his disciples — the Lord Jesus said to his disciples — "I have food to eat of which you do not know." I have food that you don't know of. You see, the disciples went off to buy food at the nearest Samaritan village, because they had run out of food. And they came back with the food and the Lord Jesus refused to eat it. And they said, "How come? Did somebody give him something to eat?" And he said, "I have food of which you don't know." Even the disciples at that stage didn't know what kind of food he was eating. And then he goes on to explain in verse 34 what that is. Jesus said to them, "My food is to do the will of him who sent me, and to accomplish his work." What is my food? My food is to do his will.

Do you ever regard the will of God as food that you can feast on? Or is it the unpleasant orders of a sergeant major? When he says, "Do this!" — "Yes, sir." What does he want me to do? What can I do? So unpleasant. All the time he says, "Do this, do that." What is your concept of the will of God? Is it as it is in the Scripture: "I delight to do your will, O God"? I delight to do it. Or is it, "Oh, since I have to do it, I am a bit scared. What is he going to tell me to do? I mean, what a fright if he says to me, 'You know that nice fat job you've got? Ditch it. Go up there to serve the Lord in China. I've got some task for you to do.'" What? I don't want to listen to the will of God. It's too dangerous. You listen to him, he's going to tell you these kind of things like, "Take up your cross." And I forget about his will. "My food is to do his will." His will is my food that I feast upon. I fill myself with his food, filled with the knowledge of his will. Is that your experience too?

You know what will be the result of that? In doing his will, you find yourself filled with all his goodness, his righteousness, such as we read of in Philippians chapter 1, verse 11 — filled with his goodness and righteousness. And also in Romans chapter 15, verses 13 and 14. Let us read this as we come to the end of this exposition. Romans 15, verses 13 and 14: "May the God of hope fill you" — notice the word. Paul is never content with anything less than filling. We are content with scraps. Paul is only content when there is filling. "May the God of hope fill you with all joy and peace in believing, so that" — notice the Holy Spirit comes — "so that by the power of the Holy Spirit you may abound in hope." Fullness. Abounding. And if that's not enough, let's go on to verse 14: "I myself am satisfied about you, my brethren, that you yourselves are full of goodness, filled with all knowledge and able to instruct one another."

You know what these points are? A church that is filled is able to instruct one another. Is able to build up one another. A church that is not filled is going to depend on one man or a few people to build them up. We need a church that is so filled that you are able to build each other up. Because out of your abundance you've got something to give to others. Then the church can grow by leaps and bounds. It has the inner spiritual strength to grow without depending on one or two or a few people. Filled. Notice how many things are filled: filled with hope — that is spiritual direction (hope in the Bible is spiritual direction, which we have been expounding before); filled with goodness; filled with all spiritual knowledge.

Which of these things are true of you? And then the Lord Jesus speaks of being filled with joy so many times. John 15:11, 16:24, 17:13 — all in John. Filled with joy. What kind of a Christianity are we reading about in the Bible? Is it your mediocre type of Christianity which only talks about getting saved? Or is it not the fullness that comes from this close communion with God? Feeding on his word. Feeding upon his will.

Have you ever tried doing God's will? Do it sometimes. The doing of God's will brings with it such refreshing strength and power. And it's just something you will delight to do more and more. For example, you find somebody hard to cope with, who has done something nasty to you. Pull your courage together, by the Lord's grace, and say to that brother — go to him, grab his hand and say, "I forgive you, and I love you, brother." You see, you're doing his will — doing God's will. You try it, and suddenly you feel yourself filled with a sense of joy and refreshing. But up to that point you refuse to forgive. Your heart is nagging and uncomfortable. "Why is he like this? Why?" And it so gets you down, doesn't it? Now you're going to do his will? Okay, no matter what he is — Christ died for this brother, he's an unpleasant fellow, but I'm going to love him with God's strength and I'm going to forgive him. You do that. Do his will, and you will find yourself refreshed, strengthened. It's a common experience. Take every challenge, do God's will, and you will know what it means to feed upon it. "My food is to do his will." You draw strength from doing his will.

One concluding point: out of your fullness, you will be able to share with others. It has a lot to do with prophecy, and I come full circle to this matter of prophecy. Everyone a prophet. You'll begin to see what we are driving at. When every Christian has this fullness — as the Scripture says, "Out of the fullness of the heart, the mouth speaks" — when you are so filled with God's word, filled with his will, filled with the Spirit, you cannot help but become a prophet. You see what he is? You can't help it. That's what a prophet is. He is filled with God's word. He is filled with God's will. You cannot help but be a New Testament prophet. That is the path to being a prophet of God.
