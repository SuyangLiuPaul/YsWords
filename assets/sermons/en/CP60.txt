# The Search for Truth: Life, Death, and the Evidence for Jesus

A few weeks ago, four young people drove a car into a garage. Two girls were 16 and 17 years old. Two boys were 18 and 19 years old. One of the boys owned this car. They drove into this abandoned garage. They closed the door. But they didn't switch off the engine. They opened all the windows of the car. They sat there and talked. And the carbon monoxide level in the garage was rising. One hour later, they were all dead. Suicide. Group suicide. This caught the attention of the people of the United States.

There are four things about this suicide that cause us to think. Suicide is something that we find very hard to handle mentally. Most people are afraid to die. But the remarkable thing about suicide is that it is a deliberate choice to die. And it is thought that most young people, because their whole life is in front of them, are afraid to die. The deliberateness of this act of death on the part of teenagers shocked the United States.

The second thing that struck people was the fact that normally, suicide is committed by one person on himself or herself. But four young people, really of their own choice, agreed as it were in a pledge to die together. Why is this so shocking?

The third thing we think about in this case is that they didn't do it because they were very poor, nothing to live on. You see, some people commit suicide because they're dying of cancer anyway. They don't want to face the end, so they take out a gun, hold it to their head, quickly squeeze the trigger and so on. In other words, they commit suicide really because they're afraid to die. They're afraid to die a painful death, so they might as well make it fast.

But these four young people were healthy young people. They were not sick. They weren't dying of any cancer. They had no financial needs. As I mentioned, one of them even owned the car, and many young people dream of owning a car one day. For him, his dream was already realized. He had these good things of life. Many young people think of having a boyfriend, a girlfriend. I mentioned these are two boys, two girls. They were already boyfriend and girlfriend. They had even this also in life.

So why did they die? It seems that for them, they could only find purpose in life through death. For them, even as teenagers, life was not about having a car or not having a car. Life was not about having money or not having money. He had money to buy the car. There was no problem there. For them, they did not have something worth living for.

The first thing we have to notice is this. If you think that suicide among healthy, normal, young people is rare, you haven't looked at the statistics. I'll give you the statistics for 1985 because the statistics for 1986 haven't become available yet.
