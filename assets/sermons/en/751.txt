They have got something that I need."

Take for example Brother Henry. I always speak of him with great affection. He was a Cantonese but he lived in Shanghai. He spoke perfect Shanghainese and it was through him that he led me to the Lord. He was a research chemist but a very humble dedicated man of God. And when the communists were coming down from the north, everybody was running for Hong Kong, for America, wherever they could go. Everybody, pastors, Christian workers, missionaries, everybody packed their bags and ran. And people said to Henry, "You better run, the communists are coming." He said, "Here I stand. This is the place where God wants me to stay. I know suffering will come, but right here I will stand. On my position here I will serve the Lord until my life is extinguished." How many men have you got today? Where do you find men of God like this today? If Henry had left, where would I be? Where would I have come to know the Lord?
