And the communists — let me tell you, as somebody who has been in China — I tell you the communists are afraid of one kind of person in China. They are afraid of the Christians. Because the Christians are the truest communists that show them to be the real hypocrites. And I mean the Christians in China. Maybe you don't practice it. But they do. The Chinese Christians don't need anybody to teach them about communism. They give to each other whatever they can. And each receives according to his needs. Nobody needs to teach them that. Some of you may have read about the Jesus Family in China. Well, you remember that those communist officials who went to see the Jesus Family were so astonished, so amazed, that here was a society far more communist than anything the communists could dream up with. And here they were doing this out of love. Nobody stuck a gun in their back. Nobody threatened them. They did this out of love.

So see the new society that God has created in the world today. And let us fight to make this society a reality. I will certainly fight for it wherever I go, whether it be in Montreal or Liverpool, wherever that may be. To build the new society. That's what I'm here to do. To build the church is to build the new society. In which everybody breaks down the barriers between each other and labors to build. To give as much as you can, to receive what you need. That's what this new society is all about. Both materially and spiritually. Not only spiritually but also materially. That is why I say let us prove it. Let us test it. I hear that this conference still has some needs, some deficits. Let us give. If you can give five dollars, give it. Let's put it to the test. Give where the need is. Because you know that in giving that you have helped some brother and sister who came to this conference who couldn't have afforded to come. That's why there was the deficit. The deficit was to meet the needs of those brothers and sisters who could not otherwise afford the cost of this conference. When will you help them? Let me tell you that I intend to give a lot more than a few dollars. I intend to take nothing out of this conference for myself.

So now remember then this: that we are serving the Lord. Each giving according to what we are able to give. But receiving each — the poorer ones — according to their need until they are able to give. Let us apply these principles. Let us see the oneness of the body of Christ. Let us realize that what we give to the other we have given to Christ. That is the truth that we have been looking at.

Let us let God then fire us when we have seen the beauty of what is ahead. The beauty of the potential of the church. Those weak and full of sinners. But yet how wonderful it can become. A reality which we have seen become a reality in Acts. And it can be a reality again today. Many people who have been to our church in Liverpool say, "We haven't seen a church like this. People love one another. There is a sense that as soon as you come you feel at home. Why is it that we can't find this wherever we go?" The secret is very simple. Because we are building the kingdom of God. We are not building just a church. We are building the kingdom of God in the world. A society in which we practice what we preach. That we give what we can. We receive what we need.

Oh, let us fight for this. In Montreal we will aim for this. And I don't intend to stay too long in Montreal, God willing. Like Abraham, move around in my tent and push on to the next place when the Lord's orders come. But wherever we go — going back to your own church — and there labor to build. And I thank God for that question: "How can I make a dead church alive?" Well, labor at it. Pray for it. Talk to the people. Encourage them. Say, "Don't you know what the scripture teaches? Let us build God's kingdom." Until they can catch the vision of it and say, "Yes, what am I doing? I should be in this too." Until the spiritual ambition is there. Say, "This must get done." And then you will see the non-Christians flowing into the churches.

Let me tell you a truth. We in Liverpool hardly have a single evangelistic meeting. Does this surprise you? We do have occasionally, very seldom. But people are coming to the Lord all the time. Why? Through the testimony of a living church. There was a hospital, and one of our sisters is here from that hospital in Liverpool, that started out with 20 Chinese nurses. Two of whom in that group were Christians. Three years later there were only two non-Christians. Seventeen or eighteen had come to the Lord of that particular group. Did we go out to the hospital outreach? Not at all. They came to the church and said, "Hey, there's something different here. What is it?" They wanted to find out. And then they found that they were drawn into the life of Christ. The church is to witness as light. As light draws the people in.

And so let us catch the vision of the spiritual ambition. Let us know then as we go on to serve the Lord in this way, how are we going to do it? We can be assured that God is going to give us the special guidance we need as we move on in the general guidance. And so we come on, as we saw, to the spiritual battles that we have to fight. The enemy is strong. But our Lord is stronger. We are realists. To be a Christian is to be a realist. You don't run away from the problem. You look the problem in the face and say, "How am I going to deal with this problem?" The Christians are not people who are impractical. They are realists. They don't live in the clouds. They live with their feet firmly on the ground. They see the job. They look the enemy in the face. And they go forward into battle knowing exactly what they intend to do. And in our weakness, relying on the power of the Lord.
