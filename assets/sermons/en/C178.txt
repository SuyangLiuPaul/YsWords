The death experiences. Maybe you've heard some people have experienced death. They were pronounced medically dead. Their heart had stopped and no more functions were to be seen. There are some people who may have died and actually come back to life. Maybe a few minutes later, sometimes a few hours later, and sometimes even a few days later. And because they came back, they call it a near-death experience. That means if they didn't come back, it wouldn't have been near. It would have been permanent.

Now, if you've gone through such an experience, you will have had some sort of taste of death. And if you came back from such an experience, you'll have had some sort of experience of resurrection. In the Bible, you have one or two people who went through this kind of experience, of having died for a time and coming back to life. The widow's son that Jesus raised. Or Lazarus. These people died and they came back to life. They understand death in a much deeper way than we would do. And yet they do not know what Jesus' resurrection was about.

There are two kinds of resurrection we must distinguish clearly in our minds. One kind of resurrection is the kind where you physically die like Lazarus did, and you are brought back to life, and you return to the kind of life you lived before. This kind of resurrection does not involve any change in your physical condition before death and after the resurrection. The resurrection body is the same kind of body he had before he was raised by Jesus.

But the resurrection of Jesus is different. Because when He rose again, His body was not the same body as what He had before. It was a body that could go through walls. So that when the disciples were gathered in a room, Jesus suddenly came into the room, though all the doors were locked. Yet it was not an ethereal body. It was not a misty, cloudy sort of body. He could eat with that body too. People could touch Him. You could feel His person. But the fact is that this body never dies again. As for Lazarus, of course he died again later. All those people who were raised back to life in the Old Testament, they died again later. But in the New Testament, when it speaks about the resurrection in the New Testament letters, it's not talking about that kind of resurrection. It's talking about the resurrection in which when you are raised again, your body is incorruptible, it will not die. That's why Jesus said, "He that believes in me shall never die."
