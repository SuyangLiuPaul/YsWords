Of course, he was convinced that he would literally blow his brains out. He knew that this would definitely blow his brains out. Well, amazing things do happen. And he did blow quite some of his brains out, as a matter of fact. They were all over the ceiling. The strange thing is: he didn't die. And then he was, of course, rushed off to the hospital. Anyone who hears the blast of a shotgun — it's very, very loud — and of course that draws attention. And they did what they could for him, and he recovered.

But the amazing thing is not that he didn't die. What happened was that his whole character was changed as a result of it. He became a very happy kind of person. You would have thought that he would have become a cabbage even if he lived. He would have become so brain-damaged as to be non-functional. But no, he recovered. The only effects left on him were that he was blind in one eye and he had a limp in the left leg. Apart from that, he recovered fully. And of course they had to put a metal plate into his mouth where the hole was.

What happened is what in medicine is called a prefrontal lobotomy. That is, in some mental cases, they perform an operation on the brain which is called a lobotomy, which then causes a character restructuring. And it seems that with the shotgun, unintentionally he performed this surgery on himself. Amazingly, he also lost all desire to drink alcohol. So his alcoholism was also cured. And he became a very cheerful, positive, outgoing kind of person.

Well, when I read this story, I thought to myself, isn't that amazing? That in attempting to kill himself, he made himself into a new person. He destroyed the old person and became new. A person who never had friends before because of his unpleasant disposition, now had lots of friends. Because now he became a friendly person, a person who would be a friend to others. The old selfishness somehow disappeared because he was willing to put himself to death.

Of course, I am not at all suggesting we should all perform this kind of surgery on ourselves. But what we can see is that unless the old self is dealt away with, we cannot really have God as friend. That is what it means to die with Christ, to be crucified with him, and so enter into a whole new way of life.
